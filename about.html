About

A MUN CONFERENCE
CURATED IN RAYFEL

Rayfelmun blends rigorous academics with modern hospitality, bringing together delegates who value purposeful diplomacy and global vision.

Our Story

Started as a visionary school project that only sends their students to local MUNs and teaches them how to MUN—to an organization that took us almost a year to complete to reach a dream we've barely dared to imagine before.

Today, Rayfelmun stands as a testament to student leadership, unwavering dedication, and the power of international cooperation. We are here to create a platform where every voice finds its resonance.
