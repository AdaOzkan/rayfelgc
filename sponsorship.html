We provide each delegate with 1 tote bag, 1 pen, 1 notebook, 1 folder, 1 placard, and 1 badge for the conference. According to our market research, the average cost of this fully equipped kit per delegate is around 100 - 130 TRY.

Covers the transfer expenses of our participants coming from within and outside Lüleburgaz.
