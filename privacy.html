Privacy Policy

"Strict Data Protection & Privacy Standards"

EFFECTIVE AS OF: AUGUST 2026

The Rayfelmun Organization Committee ("we", "us", "our") is strictly committed to protecting the privacy, security, and confidentiality of all delegates, advisors, and guests. This Privacy Policy dictates the collection, processing, and strict safeguarding of your data.

1. Strict Data Collection Protocol

During the application and registration process, we mandatorily collect the following information. Providing false or misleading data will result in immediate rejection of the application:

  • Identity & Contact: Full legal name, date of birth, phone number, and primary e-mail address.
  • Academic Affiliation: School name, grade level, and academic background.
  • Conference Data: MUN experience, committee preferences, position papers, and dietary/medical requirements.
  • Digital Footprint: IP addresses and browser configurations when accessing rayfelmun.org to ensure website security.

2. Permitted Use of Information

Your data is processed exclusively for the operational execution of the conference. We use your data strictly to:

  • Conduct background checks on applications and finalize committee allocations.
  • Transmit urgent conference updates, official study guides, and logistical details.
  • Accommodate specific medical or dietary necessities during the conference.
  • Print official placards, ID badges, and Certificates of Participation/Awards.

3. Absolute Media & Consent Rights

By registering for and attending Rayfelmun, you grant the Organization Committee an irrevocable, royalty-free, and perpetual right to use, publish, and distribute photographs, video recordings, and audio captures featuring your likeness. These materials will be utilized for official after-movies, social media campaigns, and promotional archives. Requests for the removal of group footage cannot be accommodated.

4. Zero-Tolerance Data Sharing Policy

We do not, and will never, sell, trade, rent, or distribute your personal information to third parties. Data is accessible solely by authorized Secretariat members. The only exception to this protocol is compliance with a formal legal request from Turkish law enforcement authorities or school administration (Ramazan Yaman Science High School) in the event of an emergency or disciplinary breach.

5. Data Retention & Permanent Deletion

All sensitive personal data (including dietary restrictions, medical notes, and phone numbers) will be permanently purged from our databases within 90 days following the closing ceremony of the conference. Basic records (names and awards) are retained in our historical archives.

6. User Rights & Inquiries

You reserve the right to request access to or correction of your personal data before the conference begins. For any inquiries regarding our strict data protocols, you must contact the Secretariat officially at the e-mail address provided in our contact section.
